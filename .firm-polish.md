# Firm tab: what changed, what I saw, and what is still owed

Branch `feat/firm-polish`, worktree `/private/tmp/lsat-firm`, cut from
`integration/all-features` at `cf85eff`. Eight commits. Nothing outside this
worktree was written to.

Every claim below is something I looked at in a browser against a seeded
tier-7 firm (`.qa-run/firm.db`, 420 cases, 7 of 11 Old Quarter districts
signed, 14 connections owned), at 1440px and 390px. Screenshots are in
`.qa-run/shots/`. The map and office scenes were driven in headed system
Chrome because headless Chromium's software rasteriser cannot get a frame out
of either.

---

## The seven defects

### 1. "Retainer" meant two things

Kept for the client, who really is on retainer and really does pay the
per-case fee. Everything about a district is now **standing counsel**.

- Board and ledger: "STANDING COUNSEL · 7 of 11 districts"; the seal on a held
  plot reads APPOINTED, not RETAINED; a row reads "Counsel to the shopkeepers'
  association"; the button is "Sign as counsel"; states are COUNSEL
  HELD/OPEN/LOCKED; the error is "That appointment could not be signed".
- Both intros now draw the line explicitly: "This is **not a client retainer**
  and pays no fee per case."
- The economy ledger's hint said "Retainers you sign reduce it" about rent,
  where "retainers" meant districts. Now "District counsel seats reduce it."
- A **third** meaning was hiding in the upgrade catalog, which the audit did
  not catch: seven upgrades sold "+2h retainer storage", meaning the passive
  safe. The office readout already calls it the safe, so those read "+2h safe
  storage" now. (`justice_constellation` also sold "unlimited-scale retainers",
  which was both the wrong word and an untrue one — it is 48h and a +25%
  streak cap, and now says so.)
- `game.py`'s canonical comment block over the district data, the source of
  the original phrasing, has been rewritten to match.

Not renamed: the stored field `district["retainer"]` (the counterparty, e.g.
"the duty roster") and the ledger event key `district_retainer`. Those are
data, not copy; renaming them is a migration. Every string a player reads says
counsel, and the field is rendered as "Counsel to {retainer}".

### 2. Two geographies on one screen

They are not a naming collision, they are two axes that nest, and the audit
read them as contradictory. A catalog region is the **street address the firm
occupied at one tier** (`_asset()` defaults it to `FIRM_TIERS[tier]["region"]`);
a map region is an **area spanning a run of tiers**. Every address sits inside
exactly one region.

So neither set was renamed. Instead:

- `TERRITORY_REGIONS` now publishes `tier_range`, and the ledger's region rail
  shows it: "Old Quarter · TIERS 0–4", "The Circuit · TIERS 5–6".
- The catalog filter is relabelled **FIRM ADDRESS** (it was CITY REGION) and
  its options are grouped in `<optgroup>`s by the map region that contains
  them, in tier order.
- One line sits between the two controls: "Above: the five regions of the map,
  and the districts your firm is counsel to. Here: the address the firm held at
  each tier. Every address sits inside one region."

### 3. Copy promising case flow that does not exist

Changed the wording rather than building the mechanic. A district-flavoured
matter in the daily docket is a second source of cases, and the economy is
tuned so that three to six cases buy one upgrade; that is a balance change, not
a wording fix, and it is not something to land unmeasured. The board now says
exactly the two things holding a district does — reputation floor, rent relief
— and `game.py` carries the note explaining why the third was removed.

### 4. The Firm tab's ledger opened on numbers

It now opens the way the map's copy does, and says the part the map's version
does not have to: "**Where the firm is the first call.** Sign a district's
institutions and your firm becomes their standing counsel. This is not a client
retainer and pays no fee per case. It buys standing, which holds your
reputation up from below, and a branch you are already paid to keep, which
comes off the daily lease."

### 5. Clients had no route to a case

The rate panel now ends in "Work a case at this rate →", which goes to
`/cases`. Same hand-off shape the decor, connection and rival cards already
use.

### 6. Connections were under-surfaced

Three fixes, all verified by walking the trip:

- **The card names the districts.** `asset.districts` was published all along
  and only a count was shown. The card now lists them as chips and ticks the
  ones already signed: "OPENS 2 DISTRICTS · 2 SIGNED — Quarter Courthouse,
  Wool Hall Yard".
- **Arriving on the map marks them.** `?connection=local_bar` used to open the
  right region and highlight one district. It now marks every district that
  connection opened and the board says which network did it: "The local bar
  association opens these 2 districts in Old Quarter." Verified against the
  live board: `marked: ["Quarter Courthouse", "Wool Hall Yard"]`.
- **The office crest.** The audit said hovering a crest gives an earnings card.
  It is worse than that: a connection carries a small fee share (+2% for the
  local bar), so it classified as `casework` and the card said "+2% of every
  case fee" and nothing else — the least of what the purchase did, on the wall
  where the purchase is displayed. The readout now also names the districts, in
  the crest's own two colours (amber for signed, dark for not), so the card and
  the wooden tabs under the seal say the same thing. Verified by driving a real
  pointer at the real crest in Chambers via the scene's DEV pick probe.

### 7. Display-only surfaces

**Trophies grant nothing, and should keep granting nothing.** A cash prize for
"complete 100 cases" retunes an economy built on three-to-six-cases-per-upgrade.
What they were missing was a reading, not a reward. `_achievement_state` now
publishes `progress: {current, target, unit}` for the eleven honours that count
something, and a locked card shows a bar and the count — "62 of 100 cases",
"$21M of $1B", "Tier 6 of 7" — instead of a padlock and a sentence about a
thing that has not happened. The two that are a yes-or-no ("Join the Orbital
Bar Association") show no bar, because "0 of 1" says less than the requirement
already does.

**The staff roster is now the control that finds the card.** Every figure on
the firm floor is a button: it clears whichever of the two catalog filters is
hiding that person's card, scrolls the card to the middle, moves focus to it,
and marks it in brass for three seconds. Candidates carry their fee on the
nameplate and the header says how many are waiting ("2 candidates are ready to
hire"). Nothing is bought from the roster — price, requirement and button stay
in one place, on the card.

## Performance

Measured on production builds of base (`cf85eff`) and branch, served side by
side against the same API and database, alternating five scroll bursts each
inside one browser so both meet the same machine load. Single-run numbers on
this host were worthless — it is also running the demo stack, two other
worktrees and a deck build, and produced 17-second frames on both builds.

| Surface | Base median / p95 | Branch median / p95 |
|---|---|---|
| Districts, 1440px | 16.7ms / 17.6ms | 16.7ms / 17.7ms |
| Districts, 390px | 16.7ms / 17.6ms | 16.7ms / 17.7ms |
| Staff, 1440px | 33.4ms / 583ms | 34.1ms / 450ms |

Districts is at 60fps and unchanged. **Staff is at 30fps on both builds** — see
the follow-ups; it is not this branch's doing.

No `backdrop-filter` and no `mix-blend-mode` were added; the route stylesheets
are still split and injected after the entry sheet. No canvas was added: the
two surfaces that wanted enriching wanted *names of things*, and a WebGL layer
on a data-dense tab that already carries two heavy scenes would have been the
expensive way to fail to say them.

## Follow-ups

**1. Twelve districts have no landmark.** All of Treaty Sea and Global Compact
(`landmark: None` in `DISTRICTS`) — so no map pin, no camera flight, no
district brief, no contact figure. Five of the fourteen connections open only
districts in those regions, which means five connection cards now name
districts that the map cannot show you. The trip still works (the board opens
and marks the rows) but the payoff is a list, not a place. Fixing it means
authoring twelve landmarks in the map's urban plan and facade tables, which are
owned by another worker tonight. Estimate: twelve landmark entries plus the
brief and contact copy for each; the machinery already exists and is exercised
by the twenty-six districts that have them.

**2. The Staff tab scrolls at 30fps, on base as well as this branch.** Each of
the fourteen roster figures is a `StylizedCharacter`, i.e. a three.js render,
and they animate whether or not they are on screen. p95 is in the hundreds of
milliseconds on both builds. The cheap fix is to stop the figures that are
scrolled out of view; the thorough one is to render each to a texture once and
animate only the hovered figure. Untouched here because it predates this work
and lives in the shared character renderer.

**3. Rename the stored `retainer` field and the `district_retainer` ledger
event** to counsel, when a migration is worth doing. Copy is consistent; the
data is not.

**4. The trophy progress bars are computed server-side per request.**
`_valuation` is called twice per `_achievement_state`; trivial today, worth
hoisting if the honour list grows.
